# CLIENT PROJECT AGREEMENT

## Muhammad Taha Professional Portfolio

This agreement is between the **Client, Muhammad Taha**, and the **Service Provider** responsible for designing and developing the portfolio website described below.

### 1. Parties

**Client:** Muhammad Taha
**Service Provider:** ______________________________

Both parties agree to proceed with the project according to the approved scope and terms in this agreement.

---

### 2. Scope

The Service Provider will design and develop a premium personal developer portfolio website for Muhammad Taha.

The website will include:

* Home / Main Portfolio Experience
* About
* Skills
* Projects
* Selected Project Detail pages
* Education
* Certifications
* Contact
* Footer

The website will include a professional visual design, responsive layouts, controlled motion, interactive elements, and a focused 3D hero experience.

The work will follow the approved Scope & Quote. Any functionality not included in that scope is not automatically part of the project.

---

### 3. Deliverables

The project deliverables include:

* Custom portfolio visual design
* Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile experience
* Portfolio sections and navigation
* Project showcase and selected project detail pages
* Skills, education, and certification sections
* Contact section and agreed contact-form functionality
* GitHub, LinkedIn, and Live Demo links
* Smooth scrolling and interactive transitions
* Hover effects and micro-interactions
* Scroll-based animation
* Cinematic transitions where appropriate
* Focused 3D hero experience
* Appropriate 3D fallback behavior
* Functional, responsive, visual, accessibility, and performance testing
* Production deployment to the client's agreed hosting environment

No admin dashboard, CMS, database-backed content management, or API-driven integrations are included in this project.

---

### 4. Timeline

The target delivery period is **3–4 working days**.

The planned schedule is:

| Day   | Primary Focus                                                                |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | Discovery finalization, visual direction, core structure, hero/3D foundation |
| Day 2 | Main portfolio sections, projects, education, skills, certifications         |
| Day 3 | Project details, contact, motion, interactions, responsive refinement        |
| Day 4 | QA, performance/accessibility checks, final corrections and deployment       |

The timeline depends on the Client providing required content, assets, decisions, approvals, and access credentials promptly.

Delays in these areas may affect the delivery date.

### 6. Revision Limits

Revisions are included within the approved design and functionality scope.

At each review milestone, the Client should provide **one consolidated set of feedback**.

Revisions will focus on correcting or refining work within the approved direction.

The following may be treated as additional scope:

* New pages
* New functionality
* CMS or admin dashboard
* APIs or new integrations
* Major structural redesign
* Replacing the agreed 3D concept after implementation
* New interaction systems
* Other changes that substantially alter completed work

Additional work will be discussed and estimated separately before implementation.

---

### 7. Client Responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate and timely:

* Professional name and positioning
* Introduction/bio
* Project information and descriptions
* Project screenshots/assets
* Technology lists
* Live Demo URLs
* GitHub repository URLs
* Education information
* Relevant coursework
* Certification information
* Contact information
* LinkedIn and GitHub URLs

The Client will also confirm the primary CTA, portfolio structure, priority projects, 3D direction, contact mechanism, and other decisions required for implementation.

The Client is responsible for reviewing submitted work and providing consolidated feedback and approvals promptly.

---

### 8. Change Requests

Requests that remain within the approved scope may be handled as revisions.

Requests that add new functionality, pages, integrations, or substantially change the approved direction may require a separate estimate.

The Service Provider will discuss such changes with the Client before starting the additional work.

---

### 9. Hosting and Domain

The Client is responsible for providing or confirming the hosting environment and domain required for deployment.

Hosting and domain purchase costs are **not included** unless specifically stated in the commercial quote.

The Service Provider will deploy the completed portfolio to the agreed hosting environment as part of the deployment scope.

---

### 10. Third-Party Services

Third-party services, paid fonts, stock assets, hosting, domain costs, and other third-party fees are not included unless specifically stated in the commercial quote.

The Client is responsible for providing access or credentials where required for agreed services.

The specific contact/email delivery mechanism will be finalized before implementation.

---

### 11. Approval Process

The project contains four approval milestones:

1. **Discovery Finalization**
2. **Visual Direction & Core Experience**
3. **Portfolio Implementation**
4. **QA & Deployment / Final Approval**

The Client will review the work at the relevant milestone and provide consolidated feedback.

Approval at each milestone allows the project to proceed to the next stage.

---

### 12. Final Delivery

Final delivery will occur after:

* Included functionality has been completed
* Agreed revisions have been addressed
* Testing and final corrections have been completed
* The portfolio has been deployed to the agreed hosting environment
* Final approval has been provided

The final delivery is limited to the functionality and content included in the approved scope.

---

### 13. Maintenance After Delivery

Ongoing maintenance after the agreed delivery period is **not included** in the initial project scope.

Future maintenance, updates, new content, new functionality, or additional improvements may be discussed and quoted separately.

---

### 14. Cancellation

If either party needs to stop the project before completion, both parties should communicate this promptly and discuss the work completed and the applicable outstanding project arrangements based on the agreed payment milestones.

No additional cancellation terms are established by this agreement beyond the agreed project and payment arrangements.

---

### 15. Intellectual Property

The completed portfolio work produced specifically for this project will be delivered to the Client as part of the final project delivery, subject to the agreed project payments being completed.

Third-party materials, services, fonts, assets, or software remain subject to their respective terms and licenses.

---

### 16. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential project information shared during the project and not disclose it unnecessarily to third parties.

This includes private project information, credentials, unpublished content, and other information that is reasonably understood to be confidential.
